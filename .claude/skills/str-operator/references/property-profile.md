1. **Multi-gen family holidays** — grandparents + parents + kids, Christmas / school holidays / milestone birthdays. Repeat-rate is high, low price-sensitivity, long stays. **Highest LTV segment.** Reviews confirm this is already the core.

3. **Milestone birthdays / small weddings (40th, 50th, 70th, 80th)** — reviews already show this. Weekend peak.

## Known gaps (opportunities)

These are the things to prioritize when asked "what should we improve". In rough order of leverage:

1. **No instant booking / direct payment.** Inquiry-only means JR is the conversion bottleneck. Every hour of delay costs bookings.
2. **No guest segmentation in copy.** Homepage speaks only to families. Corporate / golf / milestone-birthday groups bounce.
3. **No SEO landing pages.** Site is essentially one page. No "Mornington Peninsula family beach house", "Tootgarook holiday rental", "Peninsula golf group accommodation" etc.
4. **No blog / content engine.** Local-area expertise is a huge organic moat for STRs and the site doesn't exploit it.
5. **No email capture / remarketing.** Past-guest list is the single most valuable asset and there's no mechanism to build it on the site.
6. **No Google Business Profile** (verify — most STRs don't have one, and GBP is a significant search win for "near me" queries even for holiday rentals).
7. **Manual inquiry handling.** A contact form → gmail inbox is a 2010 workflow. Should be form → N8N → CRM + auto-reply + calendar hold + quote generation.
8. **No review generation loop.** 120 reviews from 1000+ guests/year is a ~12% review rate — low. Automated post-stay review requests can double this.
9. **Pricing appears to be manual.** Dynamic pricing (PriceLabs, Wheelhouse, Beyond) typically lifts RevPAN 10–20% on properties this size.
10. **OTA descriptions likely duplicate.** Airbnb, Booking.com, and VRBO reward unique, platform-tuned copy. Duplicate listings get ranked down.

## Non-obvious facts worth remembering

- The property is *beachfront across a road* — not directly on the sand. Be precise in copy; guests who expect direct sand access and find a road will review poorly.
- Mornington Peninsula peak is roughly **mid-Dec through late Jan** + Easter + Melbourne school holidays. Shoulder is Feb–Apr and Sep–Nov. Winter is soft except for hot springs / golf / food-and-wine travellers.
- Competing luxury 6BR rentals on the Peninsula: expect $1,500–$3,000/night peak. JR should know actual number; ask if pricing strategy comes up.
- The "solar-heated pool" means pool is usable most of the year — this extends bookable season and should be emphasized in shoulder-winter marketing.
- "Jason" is the name guests see in reviews. If copy mentions the host, it's Jason, not JR.
